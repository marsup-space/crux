# Code of Conduct

Crux is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, especially when disagreeing.
- Focus feedback on the work and its impact, not on the person.
- Assume good intent while remaining clear about technical concerns.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Help keep issues and pull requests focused and useful.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized conduct,
doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior is harmful to the community. Enforcement
decisions will consider context, severity, and patterns of behavior.

To report conduct privately, contact the repository owner through a private
channel listed on their GitHub profile. Do not include sensitive personal
information in a public issue.
